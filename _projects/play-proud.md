Play Proud is a program of [Common Goal](https://www.common-goal.org/) that
offers LGBTQ+ inclusion training to soccer coaches and community groups. In
2022 I went to Monterrey for the second part of the residency as a
representative of the San Diego Sirens, the supporters group for San Diego
Wave FC.

## Who was in the room

Eight clubs, five leagues, three countries:

- Angel City FC (NWSL)
- San Diego Wave FC (NWSL)
- Chicago Fire (MLS)
- Philadelphia Union (MLS)
- Oakland Roots (USL)
- San Diego Loyal (USL)
- Tigres de la UANL Femenil (Liga MX Femenil)
- Pacific FC (CPL)

Each club sent three people — someone from the team, someone from a supporters
group, and someone from a community organization. That structure is the whole
point. Inclusion work fails when it lives in only one of those three places.

## What it was

Five days of intensive workshops on relationship building, personal growth,
leadership training, and strategies for success. Less lecture than practice:
you leave with people you can call, not a binder.

## What came out of it

The Play Proud San Diego Collective — Cuyla Coogan (San Diego Loyal), Jerry
Jimenez (San Diego Wave), Lupe Flores (Chicano Federation), Nichole Peterson
(the Locals), Sulema Felix (YMCA), and me (San Diego Sirens).

Our why: to build a unified, diverse, equitable, and inclusive soccer community
that looks like San Diego.
