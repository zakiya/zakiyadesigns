A "this is me" talk for a new team. It's the personal one — the through-line
from a kid who hid good math grades from her classmates to an engineering
manager in state government.

## The short version

**Harrisburg, PA** (born in NYC). Played viola and guitar, sang in chorus, got
great grades in math and science and hid it from my classmates.

**Baltimore, 1995–2004.** Graduated from Towson University. Got involved in the
punk scene. Worked at the Charles Theatre, the Baltimore Zoo, and Donna's
restaurant. Got my first engineering job. Taught high school art.

**San Diego, 2004–2014.** Tried to save the world. Discovered hiking, biking,
and soccer. Founded the San Diego Alliance for Marriage Equality. Learned to
code and joined the Drupal community.

**The East Bay.** Worked at a Drupal shop, kept up the activism sort of, played
some roller derby. Hosted asylum seekers, moved three times, wrote a play,
rediscovered reading for joy, started an herb garden, got a cat.

## The point

None of this matters if we're not having fun.
