I'm an engineering manager at the [California Office of Data and Innovation](https://innovation.ca.gov),
where I work on digital services for the people of California.

Before ODI I was a senior Drupal developer at Chapter Three, and before that at
Workhabit and Sage Tree. Most of my career has been spent in the front end —
theming, design systems, and the unglamorous work of making a page load fast and
read clearly.

I taught full-day Drupal theming trainings for years, and I've spoken at
DrupalCon, BADCamp, SANDCamp, Drupal North, and a handful of other camps. See
[speaking](/speaking/) for the full list, and [work](/work/) for the sites I've
built.

Outside of work I organize with the San Diego Sirens, the supporters group for
San Diego Wave FC, and I've done LGBTQ+ inclusion work in soccer through Common
Goal's Play Proud program.
