In 2022, I participated in the Play Proud residency in Monterrey, Mexico. Play Proud offers LGBTQ+ inclusion training to
soccer coaches and community groups.

The experience included 5 days of intense workshops. We covered relationship building, personal growth, leadership
training, and advocacy strategies.
