## Intro

I’ve been thinking a lot about how social media interrupted the way we communicate with one another. I don’t believe any
technology is inherently good or evil. Yet the particular and increasing power that billionaires and marketers have in
our human interactions is unsettling.

Also, none of the social media platforms I’m active on can contain the massiveness of this year. I struggled. I soared.
I loved. I rested. I watched massive amounts of soccer. I tried to bust out of my comfort zone at every turn and it was
deeply painful at times. But I wouldn’t trade this year for any other.

Here’s a summary of the last 365 days in a format and a place that I can actually find in the future when I’m feeling
sad or reflective or forgetful.
