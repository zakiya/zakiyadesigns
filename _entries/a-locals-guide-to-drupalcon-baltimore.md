I’m really excited that [DrupalCon](https://events.drupal.org/baltimore2017) is in Baltimore this year. I had the
pleasure of spending my 20's roaming the streets
of [Charm City](http://articles.baltimoresun.com/1995-07-18/news/1995199190_1_charm-city-bill-evans-loden), and I still
love it more than any place I’ve ever lived. While *[The Wire](http://www.hbo.com/the-wire)* is an amazing show that
exposed the real problems that plague Baltimore, it left out the joy, art, music, and general quirkiness that wins
people’s hearts.

Here’s are some suggested things to do to while you’re spending your week in
the [Greatest City in America](http://pre00.deviantart.net/9511/th/pre/f/2012/071/e/9/baltimore_is_the_greatest_city_in_america_by_darkphoenix36-d4sjder.jpg).
The list is by no means exhaustive. I tried to limit it to places that are tourist-friendly without being overly
touristy. Most are pretty close the the Convention Center and Inner Harbor, where the the convention and hotels are
located. [Here's a handy map for reference](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1GVvklPgREnNBBsGOfjNgFp7tnOY&ll=39.28257379999998%2C-76.59996660000002&z=12).

## Day Time Activities

[American Visionary Art Museum](http://www.avam.org/) - I’ve been to art museums all over the country but the Visionary
is my favorite. The art is from untrained artists and tends to be more whimsical, intricate and tragic than what you’d
see in a typical museum. To add to the fun, you can take a water taxi from the Harbor to the AVAM.

[Baltimore Museum of Art](https://artbma.org/) - The BMA features an impressive collection of modern and contemporary
artwork. You’ll probably have to take a cab to get there.

[The Walters Art Museum](https://thewalters.org/) - The Walters is a bit closer to the Convention Center. The
exhibitions have more classical and ancient pieces than what you’d find at the BMA. You can get to the Walters for free
by taking the Purple [Charm City Circulator](http://www.charmcitycirculator.com/) from the Inner Harbor.

[National Aquarium](https://aqua.org/) - I’ve actually never been, but people love it!

[Washington, DC](https://www.google.com/webhp?sourceid=chrome-instant&ion=1&espv=2&ie=UTF-8#q=baltimore+to+washington+dc)
is only 40 minutes and $8 away by MARC train.

## Night Time Places

[Fells Point](http://www.baltimoresun.com/bal-fellspoint-story-story.html) - In truth, you can visit Fells in the day,
do some shopping, go to Broadway Market, and sit by the pier. But Fells’ claim to fame is that it is believed to have
more bars per mile than any place in the US. Hit this up earlier in the week to avoid the college kids.

[Club Charles](http://www.baltimoresun.com/features/best-of-baltimore/bal-best-of-baltimore-best-jukebox-story.html) - I
used to work at the movie theater across the street from this too-hip-to-believe restaurant and bar. Yes, I’ve seen John
Waters here.

[Brewer’s Art](http://www.thebrewersart.com/) - Before craft brews were all the rage, this old Baltimore brewery was
serving up amazing beer. Upstairs is a nice sit-down restaurant and downstairs is a dive bar. You’ll see their signature
Resurrection Ale on tap all over town. Getting there is straight shot up Charles Street.

[Federal Hill](https://www.yelp.com/search?cflt=bars&find_loc=Federal+Hill%2C+Baltimore%2C+MD) - Federal Hill is another
neighborhood that's good for bar-hopping and it's a quick cab ride from the Convention Center.

## Nice Restaurants I’ve Never Been To

To be honest, when I lived in Baltimore I was more concerned with cheap eats than fancy ones. But here are some
restaurants that people who still live there recommended. They're in order of most mentions to least:

[Woodberry Kitchen](https://www.woodberrykitchen.com) - Not really close to the Harbor, but highly recommended by almost
everyone I asked. If you go here, take the time to stroll through Hamden.

[Tio Pepe](http://www.tiopepebaltimore.com/) - Spanish and Mediterranean according to the website.

[The Prime Rib](http://www.theprimerib.com/baltimore-md/) - I’m assuming they have Prime Rib.

[Sagamore Pendry Baltimore](https://www.pendryhotels.com/baltimore/) - My friend says it’s “the newest fanciness in
Fells.”

[Charleston](http://www.charlestonrestaurant.com/) , [Ouzo Bay](http://www.ouzobay.com/), [Azumi Restaurant](http://www.azumirestaurant.com/baltimore/)
are all close to the Drupal activities.

## Crabs

[Captain James](http://captainjameslanding.com/) - Maryland Crabs with Old Bay seasoning are a Baltimore staple. I
became a vegetarian before I moved to Baltimore so I recommend this restaurant primarily because it is shaped like a
boat.

## Notable Movies/TV Shows Filmed in Baltimore That Aren’t *The Wire*:

The city isn’t that big so you’ll probably see familiar shots from:

- *Hairspray*
- *Cry-Baby*
- *Roc*
- *Ace of Cakes*
- *House of Cards*
- *Diner*
- *Liberty Heights*
- *Veep*

If you’re going to DrupalCon Baltimore I hope you get to explore the city beyond the Inner Harbor. If you see me around,
I’ll happily talk your ear off with many more recommendations. (Also, check out my
session [15+ Ways to Debug Drupal 8!](https://events.drupal.org/baltimore2017/sessions/15-ways-debug-drupal-8-front-enders))
There are great clubs for punk and hip-hop, plenty of LGBTQ-friendly spots, great old movie theaters, weird public art
and history, history, history. With an open mind and a bit of an adventurous spirit, hopefully you’ll fall a little bit
in love with the city too.
